Patient Room VR

See the room as the patient: sitting up in bed or lying down. The ceiling, fillet and foot wall carry the printed landscape.

1 · Ceiling picture

“Original room” is the unedited room (tile ceiling), for comparison.

2 · Patient position

Lying, seated user: looking straight ahead shows the ceiling above the bed. Lying for real: lie on your back, feet pointing where you were facing when you pressed Recenter.

3 · VR headset optics

Tune with the grid: the two images should merge into one, and straight lines should stay straight up to the edge. If the lines bow outward, raise k1/k2; if they bow inward, lower them. Measure your screen width (the long side of the lit area) once with a ruler, because millimetres on the screen depend on it.

Comfort ratings

In VR, put the gaze dot on a star below the view and press the headset button to rate how comfortable each ceiling feels.

Headset button: press it anywhere to recenter the view. To use the menu, look below the view (toward your feet when lying for real) until the gaze dot is on a button (the ring grows), then press the headset button to click it.
